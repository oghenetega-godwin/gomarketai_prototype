OPay NICA 2026 Core Workflow

This prototype resolves standard carrier USSD timeout issues (20-second threshold) using an **asynchronous micro-session** pipeline.

1

Dial & Instantly Drop: Smallholder dials `*356#`. The session drops in 1.5 seconds, avoiding cellular timeouts. The cloud gateway immediately calls the farmer back over stable GSM audio lines.

2

Gemini Voice Core: The farmer records their listing. Google Gemini parses regional dialects, translation layers, and currencies asynchronously without requiring an active browser connection.
